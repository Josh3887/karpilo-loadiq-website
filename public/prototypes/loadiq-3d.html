The problem

The rate on the board isn't what you keep.

A load's advertised rate says nothing about your settlement split, the empty miles to get there, the fuel you'll burn, or the reserve your truck needs. LoadIQ runs those numbers before you say yes.

Intelligence · 01 of 06

Fuel surcharge is fuel money. Not profit.

LoadIQ projects gallons and fuel expense from your MPG and diesel price across every mile, loaded and empty, then shows whether the surcharge actually covers it.

gallons = total miles ÷ MPG
fuel expense = gallons × diesel price
net fuel position = surcharge − fuel expense
Intelligence · 02 of 06

Every mile turns the wheels. Not every mile pays.

Rates are quoted on loaded miles. LoadIQ separates loaded from deadhead and measures revenue per total mile, so a strong-looking rate with a long empty run shows its real value.

Origin

Built from the driver's seat.

LoadIQ is built by an over-the-road flatbed driver who knows settlements, percentage pay, fuel surcharge, tarps, deadhead and maintenance reserves firsthand. It's made for drivers and owner-operators who want to know what a load is worth before they commit.

Straight talk

What LoadIQ is, and isn't.

LoadIQ is

  • A freight profitability calculator
  • Fuel, deadhead and cost intelligence
  • Saved-load financial snapshots
  • Financial education for drivers

LoadIQ isn't

  • A broker, dispatcher or load board
  • A source of live freight rates
  • Turn-by-turn navigation
  • A guarantee of any financial result
