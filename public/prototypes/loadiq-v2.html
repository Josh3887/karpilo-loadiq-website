Demonstration settlement convention

Carriers pay drivers and owner-operators differently. This page uses one stated convention so the math is transparent. It is an adjustable assumption for this demo, not a universal LoadIQ rule and not your carrier's formula.

Driver-attributable revenue = linehaul × compensation % + fuel surcharge
Estimated profit = driver-attributable revenue − fuel − maintenance reserve − other trip expenses

Estimated profit is before income tax and before any costs you leave out. It is not take-home pay.

Fuel intelligence

Fuel surcharge offsets fuel. It isn't profit.

Fuel expense scales with every mile you run, loaded or empty. Fuel surcharge is usually paid on the load. Where the two lines cross is the diesel price at which surcharge only just covers your fuel.

Deadhead intelligence

The rate is quoted on loaded miles. You pay for all of them.

Deadhead is the empty run to the shipper. Revenue stays the same, so every empty mile spreads the same dollars across more road and adds fuel and wear on top.

Straight talk

What LoadIQ is, and what it isn't.

LoadIQ is

  • A freight profitability calculator for drivers and owner-operators.
  • A way to compare loads by estimated profit, not the posted number.
  • Financial education: deadhead, fuel surcharge, settlement splits, reserves.
  • Built by an over-the-road flatbed driver who runs these numbers himself.

LoadIQ is not

  • A freight broker, dispatcher, load board, or load-matching service.
  • A source of live market rates or freight listings.
  • Turn-by-turn navigation or truck routing.
  • A guarantee of any financial outcome, or tax advice.

About this page

  • Every figure is sample data, editable by you, and stays on this page.
  • The settlement convention is a stated demo assumption.
  • Route geometry, mileage, and drive time are illustrative.
